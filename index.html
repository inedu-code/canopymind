The Challenge: Traditional broadcast spraying wastes 80-90% of pesticides on bare soil, crop plants, and non-target areas. This drives up chemical costs, harms soil health, and contributes to herbicide resistance.

The CanopyMind Solution: Our computer vision AI analyzes real-time camera feeds from autonomous sprayers and drones — distinguishing crops from weeds at 60 frames per second with 98% accuracy. It instructs equipment to fire precision nozzles only when a weed is detected, reducing chemical usage by up to 90%.
